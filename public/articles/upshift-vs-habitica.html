Habits and quests

Both apps treat habits as something to be played. Habitica does this by turning your tasks into part of a role-playing game. Upshift does it by turning habits into quests that raise your own level.

In Upshift, quests are your main daily habits you want to build long term. Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app, offering extra XP and variety. You can make one-day quests that work like a to-do list, weekly and monthly quests that run on the days you pick, quit quests that track bad habits you want to break, and roulette quests that throw random challenges at you. Explore helps you discover new habits, and smart reminders nudge you to follow through.

Upshift also supports longer-term planning. You set a goal, split it into micro-goals and keep a journal with your plan and reflections. The time tracker and focus timer show where your day goes and how productive your sessions were.

App blocking and screen time

Habitica focuses on tracking tasks and habits. It is not an app blocker, so it relies on you to keep yourself away from distractions.

Upshift includes the blocker as a core feature. The Quest Blocker keeps distracting apps blocked until you complete your daily quests. Block Now blocks apps instantly when you need to focus, Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites, and Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. Pre-built blocks such as the porn blocker are available, and each block type has its own custom overlay. With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

The blocker is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology, which Apple built with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time. If you want to see how the setup works, read how to block apps on iPhone.

Motivation: XP, levels and streaks

Both apps use progression to keep you engaged. In Upshift, every quest you complete earns XP in its category: Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and shows up on your profile with the rest of your stats. Streak and day celebrations appear when you stay consistent, and milestones unlock further badges.

The difference is what the progress represents. In Upshift your level is a record of your own habits, and your real reward is access to the apps you chose to block. There is no separate game world to maintain, which some people find more direct and others find less immersive.

When Habitica is the better choice

Habitica is the better choice if the game itself is what keeps you coming back. If you enjoy collecting, customizing an avatar and playing inside a fantasy world, that style may motivate you more than a level on a profile page. It is also a fit if you do not want an app that restricts your other apps. Upshift's gamification is lighter on theme and heavier on real-world control.
