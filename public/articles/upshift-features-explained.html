Upshift is a gamified habit tracker and app blocker for iPhone and Android. You turn habits into quests, earn XP, and keep distracting apps blocked until your important tasks are done. This page walks through every feature so you know what the app does before you install it.

Last updated November 2026.

Quests and Sidequests

Quests are the core of Upshift. They are your main daily habits, the ones you want to build for the long term.

Quests

You create quests for the habits you care about. Each completed quest earns XP, and consistent completion builds a streak. Smart reminders nudge you to finish your habits without being annoying.

Sidequests

Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app. They offer extra XP and add variety, so your routine does not get stale. Think of quests as the habits you chose, and sidequests as bonus challenges on top.

Quest types

  • One day quests work like a to-do list and can be scheduled for future days.
  • Weekly and monthly quests run on the days you pick.
  • Quit quests help you break bad habits by tracking them daily.
  • Roulette quests throw random challenges at you.

You can also browse Explore to discover new habits instead of starting from a blank page.

XP, Levels and Categories

Every quest you complete earns XP in its category: Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. That XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and shows up on your profile with the rest of your stats. For the concept behind this, read What Is Gamified Habit Tracking.

Streak Celebrations and Badges

When you stay consistent or finish your daily goals, streak and day celebrations pop up. Milestones unlock badges and achievements, which are displayed on your profile.

The App Blocker

The app blocker is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology on iPhone. You can see your total usage per app, then set up blocks with one tap.

Quest Blocker

Quest Blocker is Upshift's signature feature. It blocks distracting apps until you complete your daily quests, so your important tasks come before entertainment. Read the full explanation in Quest Blocker Explained.

Block Now

Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus, for example for a few hours.

Time Limits

Time Limits cap your daily usage for apps and websites. See How To Limit Screen Time On iPhone for a step-by-step guide.

Work Schedule

Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during your work hours, so you do not have to rely on willpower in the middle of the day.

Adult content and gambling blocks

Upshift includes pre-built blocks such as an adult content (porn) block and a gambling block. See How To Block Porn On iPhone for more.

Custom overlays

Each block type has its own custom overlay, which is the screen you see when you try to open a blocked app.

Strict mode

With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked (depending on the block type). That is the point of it: the easy way out is gone.

Goals, Micro-Goals and Journal

Habits handle the daily routine, and goals handle the bigger picture. You set long-term goals and break them into micro-goals. Each goal has a journal where you write your plan and reflections, and you get deadline reminders along the way.

Time Tracker and Focus Timer

The time tracker shows where your day goes. You can track your focus sessions and daily activity on a calendar, keep your day streak alive, and look back at your history anytime. The focus timer times your sessions so you can see how productive you were.

Friends, Groups and Leaderboards

You can add friends, share your progress, and join groups with shared challenges and leaderboards. The idea is accountability: it is harder to skip a habit when people can see your progress.

Themes and Widgets

You can pick from 9 themes for your profile and add Upshift widgets to your home screen.

Affiliate Program

Share Upshift with others and earn rewards through the Affiliate Program. When people sign up using your referral code or username, you earn credits that can be used to redeem rewards.

Privacy and Screen Time Data

Upshift is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology. Apple designed it with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time data.

Availability and Trial

Upshift is available on the App Store and on Google Play, and it offers a 7-day free trial. Details are in Upshift Pricing and Free Trial. For Android specifics, see Upshift on Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between quests and sidequests?

Quests are your main daily habits that you want to build long-term. Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app that give extra XP and variety.

Which XP categories are there?

Money, Strength, Health and Knowledge. Every quest you complete earns XP in its category and raises your global level.

Can I get around a block?

With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

Can Upshift see my screen time?

No. Upshift is built on Apple's Screen Time technology, which was built with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time.

Can I use Upshift with friends?

Yes. You can add friends, share progress, and join groups with shared challenges and leaderboards.
