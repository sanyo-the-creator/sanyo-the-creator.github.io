The Student Problem: One Minute Becomes an Hour

You open your phone to check one message and lose an hour. Studying competes with social apps, videos and games that are designed to keep you scrolling. Willpower alone is a weak defense, so Upshift moves the decision earlier: you decide what comes first, and the app enforces it.

Turn Study Tasks Into Quests

Upshift turns habits into quests. A student might create quests like these:

  • Review lecture notes (a daily or weekly quest).
  • Finish a homework set (a one day quest, which works like a to-do list and can be scheduled for a future day).
  • Read a chapter (a weekly quest that runs on the days you pick).
  • Cut a bad habit, such as late-night scrolling (a quit quest, which tracks the habit daily).

Every quest you complete earns XP in one of four categories: Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. Study quests fit naturally under Knowledge, and sports or sleep habits can sit in Strength or Health. XP raises your level and unlocks badges. Smart reminders nudge you to complete your habits without being annoying.

Block Distractions Until the Work Is Done

Quest Blocker

Quest Blocker blocks distracting apps until you complete your daily quests. If your quests are your study tasks, the apps you picked stay locked until those tasks are done. Read Quest Blocker Explained for details.

Block Now

Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus. It suits a library session or the hours before an exam.

Time Limits

Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites. If you do not want to block an app completely, a cap keeps it from eating your day. See How To Limit Screen Time On iPhone.

Work Schedule

Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during set work hours. A student can use it as a study schedule so the apps are blocked during the hours when you plan to work.

Strict mode

With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked (depending on the block type). If you know you will talk yourself out of a block at 11 pm, this is the setting for you.

To see how to block specific apps, read How To Block Apps On iPhone.

Plan Bigger Work With Goals

Long assignments and exam prep need a plan. In Upshift you set long-term goals and break them into micro-goals. Each goal has a journal where you write your plan and reflections, and you get deadline reminders along the way. That makes it easier to split a big project into steps you can finish one at a time.

Track Your Study Time

The focus timer times your sessions so you can see how productive you were. The time tracker shows where your day goes, with your focus sessions and daily activity on a calendar. It also keeps a day streak, and you can look back at your history anytime.

Stay Accountable With Friends

You can add friends, share progress, and join groups with shared challenges and leaderboards. Study partners and classmates can keep each other accountable.

Privacy

Upshift is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology. Apple designed it with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time data.

A Simple Starting Setup

  1. Create two or three quests for your most important study habits.
  2. Turn on Quest Blocker for the apps that distract you most.
  3. Add a Work Schedule for your regular study hours.
  4. Use the focus timer during study sessions.
  5. Add a friend or join a group for accountability.

Start small. Too many quests on day one is a common way to burn out, so add more once the first few feel routine. For more on motivation, read Why You Feel Unmotivated.
