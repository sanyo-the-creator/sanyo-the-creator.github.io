Habits and quests

Streaks is built around one clear idea: do a habit, keep the chain alive. That simplicity is its strength. You see your habits, tick them off and try not to break the streak.

Upshift keeps the streak but wraps more around it. Quests are your main daily habits you want to build long term. Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app that give extra XP and keep things interesting. You can create one-day quests that work like a to-do list, weekly and monthly quests that run on the days you pick, quit quests that track a bad habit you want to break, and roulette quests that throw random challenges at you. The Explore section helps you find new habits to try. Smart reminders nudge you to complete your habits without being annoying.

Beyond habits, Upshift also offers goals. You set a long-term goal, break it into micro-goals and keep a journal with your plan and reflections, with deadline reminders along the way.

App blocking and screen time

Streaks is a habit tracker, not an app blocker, so it will not stop you from opening a distracting app. Upshift treats your phone as part of the habit problem.

The Quest Blocker keeps distracting apps blocked until you complete your daily quests. Block Now blocks apps instantly when you need to focus. Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites, and Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. Pre-built blocks, such as the porn blocker, are included, and each block type has its own custom overlay. With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

Upshift is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology, so it cannot view your screen time data. To set blocks up, follow our guide on how to block apps on iPhone.

Motivation: XP, levels and streaks

Streak-based motivation works because nobody likes breaking a chain. Upshift includes streaks and adds more layers. Each completed quest earns XP in Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and appears on your profile. Streak and day celebrations pop up when you stay consistent or finish your daily goals.

The strongest motivator in Upshift is the blocker. Your entertainment apps stay locked until your quests are done, so finishing your habits becomes the way to unlock your own phone. The science behind this approach is covered in how app blockers work.

Who each app is best for

Upshift is best for

  • People who want game-like progress: XP, levels, badges and leaderboards.
  • People whose habits keep losing to social media and games.
  • People who want habits, goals, time tracking and app blocking in a single app.
  • People who want to share progress with friends and groups.

Streaks is best for

  • People who want a clean, minimal tracker with no extra systems.
  • People who are motivated purely by keeping a chain going.
  • People who already manage their screen time in another way.

When Streaks is the better choice

Streaks is the better choice if you find gamification distracting. Some people want to check a box and move on, without levels, categories or a blocker. If your phone use is not a problem and you only need a straightforward habit chain, a focused tracker is easier to live with. Upshift has more features, which is useful if you want them and noise if you do not.
