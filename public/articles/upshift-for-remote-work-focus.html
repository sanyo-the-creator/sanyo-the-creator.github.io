Upshift helps remote workers focus by blocking distracting apps during work hours and tying phone access to the habits you set. Work Schedule, Block Now, Time Limits and the focus timer give you structure when nobody else provides it. This page explains how to use them for a home office routine.

Last updated November 2026.

Why Remote Work Needs Phone Boundaries

At home, your phone sits next to your laptop all day. There is no commute, no office norms and no coworker walking past your desk. Every notification is a chance to lose twenty minutes. Instead of relying on discipline in the moment, you can set the rules once and let the app enforce them.

Work Schedule: Block Apps During Work Hours

Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. You set the schedule that matches your day, and the apps you chose stay blocked while you are meant to be working. When the day ends, the blocks end too, so your evenings are your own.

Upshift is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology on iPhone, the same system that powers the built-in blocking options. See How To Block Apps On iPhone for how blocking works in general.

Block Now for Deep Work

Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus. Use it before a deadline, a writing session or a long call prep. It is a quick one-tap option when your normal schedule is not enough.

Time Limits for Apps You Cannot Fully Quit

Some apps are both useful and distracting. Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites, so you can still use them without losing the whole afternoon. For a step-by-step guide, see How To Limit Screen Time On iPhone.

Strict Mode: No Easy Way Out

With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked (depending on the block type). Remote workers often know the moment they will be tempted to turn off a block. Strict mode removes that option.

Quest Blocker: Work First, Entertainment Second

Quest Blocker blocks distracting apps until you complete your daily quests. A remote worker can make a quest out of the first deep work block of the day, a morning routine, or an inbox check, and keep entertainment apps locked until those are done. Read Quest Blocker Explained for more.

Measure Your Day With the Focus Timer and Time Tracker

The focus timer times your sessions so you can see how productive you were. The time tracker shows where your day goes, with focus sessions and daily activity on a calendar. You can keep your day streak alive and look back at your history anytime. This helps you notice patterns, such as which parts of the day you focus best.

Build Habits Around Your Workday

Remote work is easier when the edges of the day are clear. Upshift quests can cover the habits around your job:

  • A morning routine before you start work (a daily quest).
  • A walk or workout at lunch (a quest earning Strength or Health XP).
  • Planning the next day at the end of work (a one day quest).
  • Cutting a time-wasting habit (a quit quest, which tracks it daily).

Each completed quest earns XP in Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge, and builds streaks and levels over time. Smart reminders nudge you without being annoying.

Goals for Longer Projects

For larger projects, set a long-term goal and break it into micro-goals. Each goal has a journal for your plan and reflections, and you get deadline reminders along the way.

Accountability With Friends and Groups

Working alone can make it easy to drift. You can add friends, share progress, and join groups with shared challenges and leaderboards.

Privacy

Upshift is built on Apple's Screen Time technology, which was built with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time data.

A Simple Remote Work Setup

  1. Create a Work Schedule that matches your usual hours.
  2. Choose the apps that distract you most and block them in that schedule.
  3. Add Quest Blocker for entertainment apps, tied to your morning quests.
  4. Use the focus timer for your deep work sessions.
  5. Turn on strict mode if you tend to disable blocks.
