Habits and quests

Both apps help you define habits and check them off. Habitify is known as a dedicated habit tracker, so its core experience is logging habits and seeing how consistent you have been.

Upshift calls habits quests. Quests are your main daily habits you want to build long term, and sidequests are daily challenges the app generates for extra XP. You can create one-day quests that work like a to-do list, weekly and monthly quests that run on the days you pick, quit quests to break bad habits by tracking them daily, and roulette quests that throw random challenges at you. Explore lets you browse ideas for new habits. Smart reminders nudge you to complete your habits without being annoying.

For bigger ambitions, Upshift adds goals. Set a long-term goal, break it into micro-goals and use the journal for your plan and reflections. You get deadline reminders along the way. The time tracker shows where your day goes, and the focus timer times your sessions.

App blocking and screen time

A pure habit tracker records what you did. It does not control what your phone lets you do. Habitify is a habit tracker, so it is not designed to block apps.

Upshift is. The Quest Blocker blocks distracting apps until you complete your daily quests. Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus. Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites, and Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. Pre-built blocks like the porn blocker are included, and every block type has a custom overlay. With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

Upshift is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology, so it cannot see your screen time data. Learn how to set limits in how to time limit apps on iPhone.

Motivation: XP, levels and streaks

Habit trackers usually rely on streaks and completion rates for motivation. Upshift uses streaks too, and adds a reward system. Each completed quest earns XP in Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and appears on your profile. Streak and day celebrations pop up when you stay consistent or finish your daily goals.

The blocker makes the reward concrete. Entertainment apps stay locked until your quests are done, so the habit comes first and the scrolling comes after.
