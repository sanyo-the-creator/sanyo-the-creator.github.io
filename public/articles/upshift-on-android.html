The Features Behind Upshift

Upshift is a gamified habit tracker and app blocker. Here is a summary of what the app is designed around, as described in the app overview. Availability of each item on Android should be confirmed in the app and store listing.

Habits as quests

You turn daily habits into quests and sidequests. Completed quests earn XP in Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge, build streaks and raise your level. Quest types include one day quests, weekly and monthly quests, quit quests and roulette quests.

Blocking

Quest Blocker keeps distracting apps blocked until your daily quests are done. Other blocks include Block Now, Time Limits and Work Schedule, plus pre-built blocks such as the adult content block. Strict mode makes a block impossible to bypass until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type. For how blocking is explained on iPhone, see Quest Blocker Explained and How To Block Apps On iPhone.

Goals and focus

Set long-term goals, break them into micro-goals and keep a journal. The time tracker and focus timer show where your day goes.

Social and customization

Friends, groups and leaderboards add accountability. There are 9 themes for your profile and widgets for your home screen.

For the full list, see Upshift Features: A Complete Walkthrough.
