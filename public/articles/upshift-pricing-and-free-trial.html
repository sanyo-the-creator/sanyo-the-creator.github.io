How to Cancel a Trial

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple or Google account, not inside a website. The exact menu names can vary between software versions, so treat these as general directions.

On iPhone

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Open the subscriptions section.
  4. Select Upshift and choose the option to cancel.

On Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon.
  3. Open the payments and subscriptions section.
  4. Select Upshift and choose the option to cancel.

If you want to cancel before a trial ends, do it before the trial period is over, and check the date shown in your subscription settings. If you cannot find the subscription, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used to start the trial.

Refunds

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. If you think you were charged by mistake, see Apple's page on requesting a refund: Apple refund information. For Google Play purchases, use Google Play's help pages.
