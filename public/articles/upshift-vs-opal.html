Blocking approach

Both apps exist to keep you from losing hours to your phone, but they start from different places.

Upshift's signature feature is the Quest Blocker. It blocks distracting apps until you complete your daily quests, so you earn your screen time by doing the important things first. Beyond that, Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus. Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites. Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. Pre-built blocks, such as the porn blocker, are ready to use, and each block type has its own custom overlay.

Upshift also shows your total usage per app, so you can see where your time goes before you set blocks. With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

Opal is known as a focus-first tool: you pick distracting apps and block them during focus sessions or on a schedule. Check their site for the exact options. If your need is simply to block apps for a set time, a dedicated blocker can cover that well.

Privacy and how blocking works

Upshift's blocking is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology. Apple built that technology with privacy in mind, so Upshift cannot view your screen time data, even if it wanted to. Because of this foundation, we do not claim Android blocking works the same way as on iPhone. If Android matters to you, check the Google Play listing and the FAQ. Habits and quests

This is where the two apps differ most. Upshift is a habit tracker as well as a blocker. Quests are your main daily habits you want to build long term. Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app for extra XP. You can create one-day quests that work like a to-do list, weekly and monthly quests that run on the days you pick, quit quests for breaking bad habits, and roulette quests with random challenges. Smart reminders nudge you along.

Upshift adds goals, too. Set a long-term goal, split it into micro-goals and keep a journal with your plan and reflections. The time tracker and focus timer show where your day goes and how productive your sessions were. A blocker-only app generally does not include this layer, so check Opal's site for what it offers.

Motivation: XP, levels and streaks

Each quest you finish in Upshift earns XP in Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and shows on your profile. Streak and day celebrations pop up when you stay consistent. The blocker gives that progress real meaning: finishing your quests is how you get your apps back.
