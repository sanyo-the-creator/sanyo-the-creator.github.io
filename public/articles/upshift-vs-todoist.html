Habits and quests vs tasks and projects

The two apps start from different ideas. Todoist is organized around tasks: you capture things you need to do, sort them into projects and give them dates. That works very well for errands, work to-dos and anything with a deadline.

Upshift is organized around habits. Quests are your main daily habits that you want to build long term. Sidequests are daily challenges generated by the app that give extra XP. You can create one-day quests that work like a to-do list, weekly and monthly quests that run on the days you pick, quit quests that help you break bad habits by tracking them daily, and roulette quests that throw random challenges at you. The Explore section helps you discover new habits.

So if you want a place to dump every task from work and life, Todoist is built for that. If you want to repeat the same few habits until they stick, Upshift is built for that. Upshift also has goals: you set a long-term goal, break it into micro-goals and keep a journal with your plan and reflections.

App blocking and screen time

This is where the two apps are least alike. Todoist is not an app blocker. It can remind you of a task, but it will not stop you from opening a social app instead.

Upshift includes a full blocker. The Quest Blocker keeps distracting apps blocked until you complete your daily quests. Block Now instantly blocks apps when you need to focus. Time Limits cap daily usage for apps and websites, and Work Schedule blocks distracting apps during work hours. There are also pre-built blocks, such as the porn blocker, and each block type has its own custom overlay. With strict mode on, a block cannot be bypassed until the app is unblocked, depending on the block type.

Upshift's blocking is built on top of Apple's Screen Time technology, which Apple designed with privacy in mind. Upshift says it cannot view your screen time data. See how to block apps on iPhone for setup.

Motivation: XP, levels and streaks

Every Upshift quest you complete earns XP in one of four categories: Money, Strength, Health or Knowledge. XP raises your global level, unlocks badges and achievements, and appears on your profile with your other stats. Streak and day celebrations pop up when you stay consistent, and milestones unlock more badges. Smart reminders nudge you to complete your habits without being annoying.

Todoist can feel motivating when you clear a list, but the reward is mostly the satisfaction of an empty inbox. If you respond well to levels and streaks, Upshift gives you more to chase. The extra push comes from the blocker: your entertainment apps wait until the important work is done. For the thinking behind this, read why you feel unmotivated.

Todoist is known for sharing projects and tasks, which suits collaboration on work. Check their site for the details of what they offer.

Who each app is best for

Upshift is best for

  • People who want to build daily habits and be rewarded with XP and levels.
  • People who lose hours to social media or games and want a blocker tied to their goals.
  • People who want goals, a time tracker and a focus timer in the same app as their habits.
  • People who stay accountable with friends and leaderboards.

Todoist is best for

  • People who need a detailed task list with projects, due dates and recurring tasks.
  • Anyone managing many work or household tasks, possibly with other people.
  • People who prefer a clean list over a game-like system.

When Todoist is the better choice

Todoist is the better choice if your day is mostly defined by tasks rather than habits. If you manage multiple projects, deadlines and shared work, a dedicated task manager will serve you better than a habit app. It is also the better pick if you dislike gamification and want a quiet, list-first tool. Upshift's one-day quests work like a to-do list, but they are not meant to replace a full project manager.

Frequently asked questions

Is Upshift a to-do list app?

Partly. One-day quests work like a to-do list and can be scheduled for future days. Upshift is mainly built for habits, goals and blocking distractions, not for managing large projects.

Can Todoist block apps like Upshift does?

Todoist is a task manager and is not an app blocker. Upshift's Quest Blocker, Block Now, Time Limits and Work Schedule are built specifically to block apps.

Is Upshift available on Android?

Yes. Upshift is available on the App Store and on Google Play. Its blocker is built on Apple Screen Time, so see the FAQ for details.
